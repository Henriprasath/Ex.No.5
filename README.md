# OUTPUT

## Test Scenario 1: Creative Story Generation

### Naïve Prompt

**“Write a story.”**

### Output

ChatGPT generated a short general story. The story was understandable, but it did not have a clearly defined theme, characters, conflict, or moral lesson.

### Refined Prompt

**“Write a short moral story about a college student who fails an examination because of poor time management. Show how the student changes their study habits and succeeds in the next examination. Include a clear beginning, middle, ending, and moral.”**

### Output

ChatGPT generated a well-organized story with a student as the main character. The story included the student's failure, realization of the problem, improvement in time management, and success in the next examination. It ended with a clear moral about discipline and hard work.

### Result

The refined prompt produced a more meaningful, organized, and complete story than the naïve prompt.

## Test Scenario 2: Factual Explanation

### Naïve Prompt

**“What is Artificial Intelligence?”**

### Output

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

### Refined Prompt

**“Explain Artificial Intelligence in simple terms for a college student. Include its definition, main characteristics, three applications, and one real-life example.”**

### Output

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of technology that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Its characteristics include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making. AI is used in healthcare, education, transportation, and virtual assistants. For example, AI-based systems can help doctors analyze medical images.

### Result

The refined prompt provided more complete information and was more suitable for academic learning.

---

## Test Scenario 3: Summarization

### Naïve Prompt

**“Summarize cloud computing.”**

### Output

Cloud computing allows users to access computing resources such as storage, applications, and processing power over the internet.

### Refined Prompt

**“Summarize cloud computing in five simple points. Include its definition, working, service models, advantages, and one practical example.”**

### Output

1. Cloud computing provides computing resources through the internet.
2. Users can access storage, applications, and processing power remotely.
3. The major service models are IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS.
4. It provides benefits such as flexibility, scalability, and cost reduction.
5. Google Drive is an example of a cloud-based service.

### Result

The refined prompt produced a concise and well-organized summary covering all the required areas.

---

## Test Scenario 4: Recommendation

### Naïve Prompt

**“How can I improve my studies?”**

### Output

Students can improve their studies by creating a timetable, studying regularly, taking notes, and revising important topics.

### Refined Prompt

**“Suggest five practical study techniques for a college student preparing for semester examinations. Include an explanation and a simple example for each technique.”**

### Output

1. **Create a Study Schedule** – Divide subjects into manageable study sessions.
2. **Active Recall** – Test yourself instead of only reading the notes.
3. **Practice Questions** – Solve previous questions to understand the exam pattern.
4. **Short Revision Sessions** – Regularly revise previously studied topics.
5. **Take Proper Breaks** – Use short breaks to maintain concentration.

### Result

The refined prompt generated specific and practical recommendations instead of general advice.

# Overall Findings

From the experiment, it was observed that the **quality of ChatGPT's response depends greatly on how the prompt is designed**.

Naïve prompts are useful when a quick or general answer is required. However, they may not provide enough information for complex tasks. Refined prompts give ChatGPT additional context and clear instructions, resulting in responses that are more **specific, detailed, structured, and useful**.

The experiment also showed that refined prompts are especially useful for **academic assignments, technical explanations, summaries, and recommendation-based tasks**. For simple questions or creative brainstorming, naïve prompts can sometimes produce satisfactory results.

# Conclusion

The experiment successfully demonstrated the difference between naïve and refined prompting patterns. Refined prompts generally produced better results because they clearly specified the **topic, context, requirements, and expected output**. Therefore, using well-structured prompts is an effective way to obtain more accurate, relevant, and detailed responses from ChatGPT.
